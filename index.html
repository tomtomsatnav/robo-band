About

Robo formed in York in 2023. Frank and Eric have been writing songs together since they were three; Holly and James complete the line-up.

Three vocalists, more than one songwriter, and harmonies that go where you don't expect. Too arty to be indie, too poppy for conventional art-rock. They've sold out two York headline shows, toured the UK twice, and are now signed to 25 Hour Convenience Store, the label run by The Libertines' Gary Powell.
